AI CONNECTION
Checking configuration…
Analysis sends supplied job text and verified profile facts to the configured AI provider. The key stays server-side. Recorded facts and application statuses remain unchanged.
Currency conversion: Frankfurter / ECB daily reference rates. Foreign hourly salaries require explicit weekly hours; uncertain gross pay or guaranteed hours stays in Review.

      
HOW RECOMMENDATIONS WORK
Three clear outcomes
HIGH PRIORITY
Reviewed employer evidence, explicit applicant-country eligibility, fully remote work, confirmed gross minimum, and at least 75% verified requirement coverage. Required qualifications must be covered.
REVIEW
Missing salary, uncertain location, incomplete employer evidence, missing experience, or outdated analysis. Likely eligibility stays in Review.
EXCLUDE
Evidence identifies an agency, domestic headquarters, geographic exclusion, hybrid/on-site work, part-time role, or gross maximum below the salary minimum.
Employer sources are excerpts you supply and confirm as official; the app does not fetch or independently authenticate them. AI can misread text. Inspect quotes and originals before making decisions.
